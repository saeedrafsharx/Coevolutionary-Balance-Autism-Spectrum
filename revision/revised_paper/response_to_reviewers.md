Dear Editor and Reviewers,

Thank you for identifying methodological and reporting concerns. We audited the
original notebooks and rebuilt the analysis using downloaded PCP derivatives,
explicit demographic/coverage criteria, anatomical network assignments, untrimmed
raw adjusted primary models, checked ComBat sensitivities, and site-held-out
prediction. The corrected analysis does not support the submitted ASD-specific
efficiency claim or diagnostic utility. We report uncertainty and inconclusive
equivalence tests rather than interpreting nonsignificance as proof of no effect.
The changes affect the scientific analysis, not only presentation.

## Editor: preprocessing may require reprocessing

**Partly addressed; critical provenance item open.** The original notebooks locally
computed a fixed-TR spectral power ratio from filtered regional series, despite

preprocessing and voxelwise fALFF are upstream derivatives. This removes the local
fixed-TR ratio from the revised features, but does not by itself prove the
historical PCP input branch was unfiltered. The working Methods explicitly mark
this unresolved. We will not state that the editor's concern is fully resolved
without release-specific evidence or a justified independent recomputation.

### 2. Cohort/site counts, per-site H, and raw adjusted inference

**Addressed.** The 182-person requested pool is distinguished from demographic
eligibility (140; 69 ASD/71 controls), common-node feature computation (all 140),
and the minimum-site-size adjusted model (136; 67/69). The flow figure replaces
the unexplained initial 200-person account. Three small sites are omitted from
primary adjusted inference, leaving nine sites; this is a new verified cohort,
not an attempt to preserve the previous 11-site description.

S1 lists site/group counts. S2 includes per-site/group raw and harmonized H means,
SDs, and participant manifests. The primary unharmonized H regression includes
diagnosis, site, FD, age, and IQ. GSR/no-GSR diagnosis coefficients are 1.432
(95% CI −17.173 to 20.038) and 2.056 (−12.387 to 16.499), both Holm p=1.00.
CALTECH contains only ASD participants. Excluding it leaves 127 participants and
similar imprecise estimates (1.115 and 1.660, both Holm p=1.00). Full-rank checks,
leverage, Cook's distance, and descriptive site diagnostics are retained. We
withdraw the claim that protecting diagnosis guarantees preservation of biology
or proves site variance caused the originally reported contrast.

### 3. Analysis order, leakage, trimming, and FD

**Addressed with explicit limits.** Raw untrimmed adjusted features define primary
inference. ComBat is an exploratory group-analysis sensitivity, with categorical
diagnosis and continuous age/IQ/FD explicitly protected, and has no raw fallback.
It is not used for prediction. Imputation, scaling, selection, and tuning are
nested inside site-held-out training folds. We qualify prediction as conditional
on a cohort-wide QC/node mask rather than calling the entire workflow leakage-safe.

The original-code audit found pooled raw-energy trimming, not diagnosis-specific
trimming; we clarify this discrepancy rather than reproduce an inaccurate account.
The new pooled 1.5-IQR trimming sensitivity is separate from untrimmed inference
and is never used to select the prediction sample. Table 1 reports mean FD by
group (.081±.044 mm ASD, .073±.032 mm control); FD is a covariate. Both pipelines
use the same primary participants and nodes.

### 4. Construction-related efficiency correlations and group specificity

**Addressed; interpretation changed.** The Methods derive the uniform-edge-shift
identity for H, with the actual state counts and graph size. Within-group partial
correlations control mean Fisher FC, FD, age, IQ, and site. ASD global-efficiency
partial r is .017 with GSR and .077 without GSR; neither survives correction.
The diagnosis-by-efficiency interactions are also nonsignificant (Holm p=.635
and .940). The original descriptive H-versus-all-metrics question is retained separately,
with raw and matched raw/ComBat outputs and explicit per-group correction. We
reproduced all 364 original exported H correlations and FDR probabilities,
including the original no-GSR ASD H--efficiency result (r=-.324, q=.00592, n=80).
Its absence in the revised feature/cohort definition does not establish that
the original association was false. The claims of a robust ASD-specific trade-off
are removed because no corresponding group interaction is established. Dependence
between summaries derived from the same matrix is discussed as a limitation.

### 5. Participant null scores and spatial constraints

**Addressed in part; limitation retained.** S2 contains individual null means, SDs,
z-scores, lower-tail Monte Carlo probabilities, and state counts from 1,000
permutations. We added network/hemisphere-block state permutations alongside
unrestricted permutations. A separate main-text baseline now shows all 71 male
controls against their own null expectations, with a paired figure, summary table,
and individual z-score plot. Control mean z is −4.690/−5.159 for unrestricted
states and −2.313/−2.760 for blocked states (GSR/no-GSR). Individual exploratory
BH correction spans all 284 control/pipeline/null comparisons. The full signed
weighted FC matrix remains fixed; no rewired-network null is implied.
We explicitly state that these blocks preserve coarse organization and do not
preserve full spatial autocorrelation. We do not present this as a full spatial
null or independent biological validation. A full spatial-autocorrelation null
remains a limitation, with relevant methods cited.

### 6. Effect sizes, uncertainty, sensitivity power, and equivalence

**Addressed.** Primary estimates include HC3 intervals, and descriptive unadjusted
Hedges' g includes bootstrap intervals. Approximate two-sample 80%-power MDE is
.484 at alpha=.05 and .534 at alpha=.025 for the corrected model sample; this
approximation does not model site/covariate adjustment. Equivalence tests using
exploratory ±.2 observed pooled-SD bounds are inconclusive (p=.193/.223). These
bounds are not claimed to be clinically established. We no longer argue that
power for d=.5 rules out meaningful autism-related effects.

### 7. fALFF branch, truncation, TR, regressors, and GSR rationale

**Partly addressed; historical fALFF verification open.** Downloaded FC series are
used at their supplied lengths (116–296 volumes); we apply no additional fixed
truncation. TR is participant/site-specific and includes 17 eligible scans at
3 s, so a universal .25-Hz denominator is inappropriate. PCP documentation lists
24 lagged/squared motion terms, five white-matter/CSF components, linear/quadratic
trends, and global signal only for the GSR branch. Both strategies are reported
symmetrically; GSR is not treated as a biologically superior reference.

Historical fALFF branch, exact definition, and band bounds remain to be verified.
Selecting a `nofilt` map folder and consulting current C-PAC code do not establish
the historical computation. No claim of verified unfiltered input is made yet.

## Reviewer 2

### 1. fALFF calculated after band-pass filtering

**Partly addressed; not yet closed.** The audit confirms that the old local
spectral ratio used filtered regional series and a fixed TR. Those values are
excluded from the replacement analysis. We now aggregate downloaded PCP fALFF
maps, independently from the FC calculation. However, the historical provenance
of those maps still needs release-specific verification. The Methods identify
the discrepancy between archived .01–.1-Hz configuration bounds and the former
.01–.08-Hz account. We will not claim that downloaded derivatives automatically
resolve the denominator/filtering concern. This point remains a submission gate.

### 2. Relative median labels versus neuronal activity

**Addressed.** The state definition is explicitly participant-relative, with ties
assigned +1. The same numerical fALFF can receive a different label across
participants; median splitting discards absolute scale. Above-median fALFF is
not interpreted as increased neuronal firing, and negative FC is not equated
with synaptic inhibition. H is a static signed alignment statistic, not measured
metabolic energy or a demonstrated evolving brain state.

### 3. Efficiency inclusion and identical feature schemas

**Addressed.** Both efficiencies are retained as complementary features in both
pipelines and across clinical correlations, PLS regression/correlation, and
classification. The primary inferential schema has 88 features: the legacy
spectral bipolarity descriptor is excluded and three within-limbic features are
structurally unavailable because only one primary limbic node remains. These
are transparent changes to the old 90/92-feature descriptions. The original
spectral descriptor is also reported as a separate exploratory adjusted group
comparison, without changing the frozen inferential/predictive families. Training-fold
variance filtering/selection may retain fewer features within a particular
model, but the starting feature schema is identical across pipelines.
